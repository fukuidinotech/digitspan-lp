DIGIT SPAN

How to Use

1. Start a test

On the Home screen, choose Forward or Backward, pick a level (number of digits), and tap Start Test.

  • Forward: enter the digits in the order they appeared. Example: 3 → 8 → 1 is “381”
  • Backward: enter them in reverse order. Example: 3 → 8 → 1 is “183”

Forward and Backward each keep their own personal best.

2. Memorize the digits

Digits appear one at a time. By default each digit is shown for 0.7 seconds, followed by a 0.3-second gap. You don’t need to touch the screen while they appear. If you choose Voice in Settings, digits are read aloud and not shown on screen.

3. Enter your answer

When the digits finish, a keypad appears. Type the digits you remember. If you make a typo, tap Delete to remove the last one. As soon as you’ve entered the full length, your answer is checked — there is no submit button.

To stop partway, tap Quit. A test you quit is not recorded.

4. Scoring rules

  • Each test has 5 rounds. The length stays at the level you picked (2–12 digits; 3 by default)
  • A correct answer doesn’t add a digit. To try a longer length, raise the level on the Home screen or in Settings
  • Each round only shows “Correct” or “Not quite” — the answer isn’t revealed
  • Digits are 0–9, and the same digit never appears twice in a row

5. Your result

You see how many of the 5 rounds you got right, and every round’s answer. Your answer is shown next to the correct one, with the wrong digits highlighted.

The highest level where you get all 5 rounds right is your digit span. When you beat your record, the result shows “Personal Best.” A typical adult forward span is said to be 7±2 digits (Miller, 1956). This app counts a level only when you get all 5 rounds right, so it is scored differently from standard tests.

Tap Try Again to start the same test right away, or Home to go back.

6. History

Open History from the Home screen to see your daily personal best over the last 14 days and a list of all your records.

7. Settings

Open Settings from the Home screen. Level, Digit Speed, and Presentation apply to your next test. Theme and Color change right away.

  • Level (digits): 2–12 digits. You can also change it on the Home screen
  • Digit Speed: 0.05–2.0 seconds (in 0.05-second steps). The gap between digits scales with the speed (0.3 seconds when set to 0.7 seconds)
  • Presentation: Screen (shown only) / Voice (read aloud only) / Both
  • Theme: System / Light / Dark
  • Color: Violet / Blue / Orange / Pink. Changes the color of buttons, your personal best, and selected items (the right/wrong colors stay the same)
  • Premium: a monthly auto-renewable subscription that turns off ads (price shown in the App and on the App Store). Buy, restore purchases, and manage (cancel) your subscription here
  • Privacy Settings: shown only to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Lets you change your ad consent later

8. FAQ

Which level should I start at?
We recommend starting at 3 digits (the default). When you get all 5 rounds right, raise the level by one on the Home screen. The highest level where you get all 5 right is saved as your personal best.

Where are my records stored?
Only on your device. There is no account or sign-in. Deleting the app also deletes your records.

How do I delete my records?
There is no way to delete individual records. To delete all of them, delete the app.

When do ads appear?
At the bottom of the Home, Result, and History screens, and as a full-screen ad when you start a test (Start Test on Home or Try Again on the Result screen; every other time). Never while you watch the digits, type your answer, or see whether it was correct. No ads at all while you have a Premium subscription.

How do I cancel Premium?
Go to Settings › Premium › Manage Subscription in the App, or the Settings app › [your name] › Subscriptions. You keep using the App without ads until the end of the period you have paid for.

Does my result tell me how good my memory is?
Your result shows how many rounds you got right at the level you chose. It reflects that moment only and can vary with how you feel and how focused you are. It is not a substitute for a medical diagnosis or any assessment of ability.
