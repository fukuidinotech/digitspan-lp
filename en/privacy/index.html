DIGIT SPAN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This is a translation of the Japanese original. If there is any discrepancy, the Japanese version prevails.

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS app “Digit Span” (Japanese name: 数字記憶テスト; the “App”) handles user information.

1. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect the following:

  • Your name, date of birth, or gender
  • Your email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Contacts, photos, calendars, microphone, or other device data
  • No account is required, and there is no sign-in

2. Information handled only on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the App. It is never sent to the developer’s servers or to third parties.

InformationPurpose
Test records (date and time, test type [Forward / Backward], and the longest length you answered correctly)Showing your personal bests, recent results, and daily history
Settings (starting length, digit duration, presentation, color theme and accent color, and the test type selected on the Home screen)Keeping your settings

If iPhone backup (such as iCloud Backup) is turned on, this information may be included in your Apple backup.

Digits are read aloud using the speech synthesis built into iOS. Speech is produced on your device, and no audio or digits are sent anywhere.

3. Advertising

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect device information (such as IP address, device type, and information about ad views and interactions). The ad SDK may also send diagnostic information (such as crashes and performance) to Google to troubleshoot ads.

The App does not use GPS or other location data. To deliver ads, Google may estimate your approximate region from your IP address.

Before the first ad is shown, we ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites through App Tracking Transparency. Only if you allow it may the advertising identifier (IDFA) be used to deliver ads. You can use every feature without allowing it, and you can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

If you live in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, before the first ad is shown we ask, through Google’s consent management tool (User Messaging Platform), for your consent to personalized ads and to storing information on, and reading information from, your device. If you do not consent, we show non-personalized or limited ads (ads may sometimes not be shown). You can change or withdraw your consent anytime in the App under Settings › Privacy Settings (see section 7).

For how Google uses information, see:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Analytics

The App does not include analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase).

5. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not hold personal information about your use of the App and does not share users’ personal information with third parties, except when disclosure is required by law. For information sent to Google through the ad SDK, see section 3.

6. Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under a higher age if your country sets one).

7. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, we provide the following information.

  • Controller: Toru Fukui (contact details in section 9). The developer does not receive personal data about your use of the App. Test records are handled only on your device (see section 2)
  • Inquiries: if you email us, your email address and message are processed to reply and to investigate issues, based on the developer’s legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) (see section 9)
  • Advertising: Google processes device information as an independent controller. Personalized ads and storing or reading information on your device are based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Delivering limited, non-personalized ads and preventing fraud are based on Google’s legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)
  • International transfers: Google may transfer information outside Europe under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses
  • Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw your consent anytime in the App under Settings › Privacy Settings. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal
  • Your rights: you have the right to access, rectify, and erase your personal data, to restrict and object to its processing, and to data portability. For information processed by Google, please contact Google directly. Inquiries to the developer can be sent to the contact in section 9
  • You have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority in your country

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law or new features. If we make significant changes, we will announce them on this page.

9. Contact

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply and to investigate issues. We do not share them with third parties, and we delete them once they are no longer needed after your inquiry is resolved.
