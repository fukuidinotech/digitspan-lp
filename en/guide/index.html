DIGIT SPAN

How to Use

1. Start a test

On the Home screen, choose Forward or Backward and tap Start Test.

  • Forward: enter the digits in the order they appeared. Example: 3 → 8 → 1 is “381”
  • Backward: enter them in reverse order. Example: 3 → 8 → 1 is “183”

Forward and Backward each keep their own personal best.

2. Memorize the digits

Digits appear one at a time. By default each digit is shown for 0.7 seconds, followed by a 0.3-second gap. You don’t need to touch the screen while they appear. If you choose Voice in Settings, digits are read aloud and not shown on screen.

3. Enter your answer

When the digits finish, a keypad appears. Type the digits you remember. If you make a typo, tap Delete to remove the last one. As soon as you’ve entered the full length, your answer is checked — there is no submit button.

To stop partway, tap Quit. A test you quit is not recorded.

4. Scoring rules

  • Tests start at 3 digits (you can change this to 2–12 in Settings)
  • Each correct answer adds one digit, up to a maximum of 12
  • You get two tries at each length. If you miss the first but get the second, you move on
  • Miss the same length twice and the test ends
  • Digits are 0–9, and the same digit never appears twice in a row

When you miss, your answer is shown next to the correct one, with the wrong digits highlighted.

5. Your result

The longest length you answered correctly is your digit span. When you beat your record, the result shows “Personal Best.” A typical adult forward span is said to be 7±2 digits (Miller, 1956).

Tap Try Again to start the same test right away, or Home to go back.

6. History

Open History from the Home screen to see your daily personal best over the last 14 days and a list of all your records.

7. Settings

Open Settings from the Home screen. Changes apply to your next test.

  • Starting Length: 2–12 digits
  • Digit Duration: 0.3–2.0 seconds (in 0.1-second steps). The gap between digits is 0.3 seconds
  • Presentation: Screen (shown only) / Voice (read aloud only) / Both
  • Privacy Settings: shown only to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Lets you change your ad consent later

8. FAQ

Where are my records stored?
Only on your device. There is no account or sign-in. Deleting the app also deletes your records.

How do I delete my records?
There is no way to delete individual records. To delete all of them, delete the app.

When do ads appear?
At the bottom of the Home, results, and History screens, and when you tap Try Again on the results screen (every other time). Never while you watch the digits, type your answer, or see whether it was correct.

Does my result tell me how good my memory is?
A result shows how many digits you entered correctly at that moment, and it can vary with how you feel and how focused you are. It is not a substitute for a medical diagnosis or any assessment of ability.
